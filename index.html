Good to know

A few things, answered.

This showcase includes a complete interactive demo journey. Prices, stock, delivery rates and campaign imagery are illustrative; no real payment or fulfilment is connected.

Can I place a real order? ＋

You can complete the full demo checkout and receive a sample order confirmation in this browser. It does not charge you, contact a warehouse or create a real order.

How does the 20% welcome offer work? ＋

Enter your email and first name in the welcome offer. The discount is saved in this browser and reflected in displayed product and bag prices. It is a local demo, not a real voucher.

Are the product prices final? ＋

Prices, product listings, sample ratings, campaign gallery images, stock labels and shipping rates are illustrative. Connect a verified catalogue, exact product photography and inventory before accepting real orders.

How do I find a collection? ＋

Use the category bar at the top. As you scroll through the collection sections, the active category underline follows your position.
